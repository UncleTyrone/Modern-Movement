# Slide

Crouch while sprinting to slide, then let go. The slide keeps the direction you entered with and loses speed as it goes. It lasts about three quarters as long as the previous coast. Dust, sparks, or splash follow the ground under you, and other players with the mod see that trail. Momentum carries into slide jumps.

A ramp or a run of stairs stretches the slide. Flat ground and a single bump do not. Hold the crouch key through a slide jump and you slide again when you land. Letting go before you land does not start another slide, and the crouch pose left over from the slide does not count as holding the key. The jump has to stay in the air for about 0.4 seconds first, so tapping jump and touching down immediately does not start another slide. Let go and press crouch again in the air to slam instead (the mod slam on Borderlands 2, Oz slam on The Pre-Sequel). Hold crouch through that slam and you slide out of the impact with the speed you had before the dive.

On co-op, CrouchedPct still times the slide and your Slide Distance (%) still scales that time. Slide Speed (%) scales how fast that coast moves and does not change the timer. Default speed is 120. Releasing forward does not stop it: the joiner and the host both keep rewriting speed along the entry direction. Releasing crouch cancels it, on a joiner the same as on the local player. Slide cancel uses the crouch key on your own machine. The host does not read that key for you. When a joiner jumps out of a slide, the host holds that same launch so the jump stays in the air.

Settings: distance, speed, indicator, audio, and camera push/shake.
