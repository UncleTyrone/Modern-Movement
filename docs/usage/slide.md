# Slide

Crouch while sprinting to slide, then let go. The slide keeps the direction you entered with until it slows down. Momentum carries into slide jumps.

On co-op, CrouchedPct still times the slide and your Slide Distance (%) still scales that time. Slide Speed (%) scales how fast that coast moves and does not change the timer. Default speed is 120. Releasing forward does not stop it: the joiner and the host both keep rewriting speed along the entry direction. Releasing crouch cancels it, on a joiner the same as on the local player.

Settings: distance, speed, indicator, audio, and camera push/shake.
