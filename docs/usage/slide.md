# Slide

Crouch while sprinting to slide, then let go. In Borderlands 2, hold crouch to slide and let go to stop. Letting go is the cancel. You do not press crouch a second time. A short hold ends when you release it. The slide can start as soon as you are sprinting. Press sprint again after you have let go of sprint and the slide ends then. The time that slide had left is not a wait. The Pre-Sequel already uses that hold. The slide keeps the direction you entered with and loses speed as it goes. It lasts about three quarters as long as the previous coast. Dust, sparks, or splash follow the ground under you, and other players with the mod see that trail. Momentum carries into slide jumps.

A slide keeps a footstep scrape on the ground until you stand up. There is no whoosh. The sound follows the ground you are on. A ramp or a run of stairs stretches the slide and keeps its speed, so the extra time is still a slide. Flat ground and a single bump do not. Hold the crouch button through a slide jump and you slide again when you land. Letting go of that button before you land does not. The crouched body left over from the slide is not a hold. The jump has to stay in the air for about 0.4 seconds first, so tapping jump and touching down immediately does not start another slide. The same hold works when you never slid first: sprint, jump, hold crouch in the air, and you slide when you hit the ground. That covers a normal landing and a slam. Pressing crouch again while you are already sliding does not start a second slide. The line under the crosshair shrinks with the slide and is gone when the slide ends. After that slide ends, crouch while sprinting starts another immediately. A slam does not lock the next slide, and there is no wait. The sprint you were already holding does not end the slide. Press sprint again after letting go of it and the slide ends then. The time that slide had left is not a wait. When the slide ends, the gun comes back up, including after a chain of jumps and slides. A tilted gun is not left behind. Turning around does not send you back along the first slide, and it does not cancel the landing. You keep the speed you had. You have to be moving at least as fast as a slide can start, and the crouch key has to still be down. Letting go, or a key the game cannot read, does not slide. Let go and press crouch again in the air to slam instead (the mod slam on Borderlands 2, Oz slam on The Pre-Sequel). That slam plays its landing. The crouch that started it does not turn into a slide. Let go, then crouch again, to slide.

On co-op, CrouchedPct still times the slide and your Slide Distance (%) still scales that time. Slide Speed (%) scales how fast that coast moves and does not change the timer. Default speed is 120. Releasing forward does not stop it: the joiner and the host both keep rewriting speed along the entry direction. Releasing crouch cancels it, on a joiner the same as on the local player. Slide cancel uses the crouch key on your own machine. The host does not read that key for you. Standing up at the start of the jump is not letting go, so other players keep the landing slide while they hold crouch. A short touch of ground before the jump has been in the air does not throw that landing away. When a joiner jumps out of a slide, the host holds that same launch so the jump stays in the air.

Settings: distance, speed, indicator, audio, and camera push/shake.
