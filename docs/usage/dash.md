# Dash

Dash a fixed distance forward, back, or sideways on the ground or in the air. Cooldown is the only rate limit unless Air Dash Only is enabled. Joiners get the same wait and the same ready hex. The short host hold keeps a joiner dash in the air if it touches the ground, and it does not rewrite how fast they are falling. An air dash leaves a wind burst and a whoosh. The whoosh is the Longbow teleport. On The Pre-Sequel the Oz air boost plays first, and the same whoosh plays if that boost is missing. The ready beep is only the cooldown finishing. A ground dash kicks up the surface you left and a shorter streak. Sprinting leaves a light streak. Super sprint does not add a whoosh. A double jump plays a jump. A slam plays only the impact when you land. Other players with the mod see the particles.

Settings: cooldown, audio, screen arrows, ready pip (On), damage reduction, perfect-dodge window and Perfect Dodge Effect, plus camera shake / lean / push sliders.
