# Dash

Dash a fixed distance forward, back, or sideways on the ground or in the air. Cooldown is the only rate limit unless Air Dash Only is enabled. Joiners get the same wait and the same ready hex. The short host hold keeps a joiner dash in the air if it touches the ground, and it does not rewrite how fast they are falling. An air dash leaves a cool wind burst. A ground dash also kicks up the surface you left. Other players with the mod see both.

Settings: cooldown, audio, screen arrows, ready pip (On), damage reduction, perfect-dodge window and Perfect Dodge Effect, plus camera shake / lean / push sliders.
