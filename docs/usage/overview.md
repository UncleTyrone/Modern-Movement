# Usage overview

Enable **Modern Movement** in the mod menu. Each feature is its own page, with an Enabled toggle and a Settings submenu. Key bindings sit at the bottom of the mod options. Check them after install.

The same package runs in Borderlands 2 and The Pre-Sequel. Double Jump, Dash, Mantle/Vault, Slide, Surface effects, Holster, and Sprint Surge load in both. Slam loads in Borderlands 2. The Pre-Sequel keeps Oz slam. You can turn any loaded feature off on its own.

## Slide

Hold crouch while sprinting, then let go to stop. Holding crouch through a slide jump slides again on landing, after the jump has been in the air for a moment. Letting go before you land does not slide. Sprint, jump, and hold crouch with no slide before it, and you also slide when you land or slam. That landing slide goes the way you are facing. The key has to still be down when you land.

The crouch check is on your own machine. The slide bar shrinks across the slide and is empty when the slide ends. A slide is the ground scrape, and it keeps footsteps until it ends.

## Slam (Borderlands 2)

Hold crouch in the air. The key is read from the Willow2 input event, so a hold still counts when the game drops it from its key list. Once the jump is high enough you slam, even if you are still running. A short hop, once it has peaked, pulls you down. Crouch has to be held, or the jump is left alone. The slam plays only its impact. Sliding is available again as soon as you land. A crouch held through a slide jump still slides on landing instead of slamming.

## Sound and effects

A dash plays a whoosh. The beep is only the ready tone. Sprinting leaves a light streak. Super sprint does not add a whoosh. A slam lands in a circle of dust. Each game uses its own effects. A missing effect is skipped.

## Co-op

A joining client sends dash, slide, and jump to the listen host, so those moves are not limited to the weapon holster. Players without the mod stay on normal movement.

A session log, `Modern_Movement.log.txt`, is written in the same folder as the mod.
